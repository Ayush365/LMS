# 📽️ PPT Presentation Reference: LMS Smart Planner

This document serves as a simplified guide for your presentation. It breaks down the project into logical slides with easy-to-understand language.

---

## Slide 1: Project Introduction
*   **Project Name:** LMS Smart Planner
*   **Tagline:** A modern Learning Management System for the next-generation student.
*   **Simple Goal:** To help students stay organized, focused, and productive by combining course management with smart study tools.

---

## Slide 2: The Problem & Our Solution
*   **The Problem:** Students often feel overwhelmed by multiple courses, deadlines, and a lack of clear study structure.
*   **Our Solution:** A unified dashboard that doesn't just list courses but helps you *plan* and *execute* your study sessions using smart timers and visual progress trackers.

---

## Slide 3: Core Module 1 - User Dashboards (Roles)
*   **For Students:** Focused on their personalized study schedule, task lists, and focus timers.
*   **For Teachers:** Tools to manage course content and provide feedback on student reports.
*   **For Admins:** A bird's-eye view of the system to monitor activity and manage users.

---

## Slide 4: Core Module 2 - Smart Course Management
*   **Organized Content:** View all your subjects, semesters, and departments in one place.
*   **Easy Navigation:** Quickly switch between different subjects to see upcoming lectures or assignments.

---

## Slide 5: Core Module 3 - Interactive Kanban Tasks
*   **Drag-and-Drop Workflow:** Move your tasks from "To-Do" to "In-Progress" and finally to "Done."
*   **Priority Tracking:** Mark tasks as High, Medium, or Low to know what to work on first.
*   **Deadlines:** Never miss a due date with built-in deadline reminders.

---

## Slide 6: Core Module 4 - Focus Mode (Pomodoro)
*   **Concentration Tool:** A built-in timer that helps you study in 25-minute blocks (Pomodoro Technique).
*   **Session Tracking:** Every study session is recorded so you can see exactly how much time you spent on a specific task.
*   **Distraction Free:** A dedicated "Focus Mode" UI to keep you away from other dashboard distractions.

---

## Slide 7: Core Module 5 - Real-time Analytics
*   **Visual Progress:** See your growth through simple charts and graphs.
*   **Performance Metrics:** Track your task completion rates and study consistency.
*   **Data-Driven:** Helps students identify which subjects need more time and effort.

---

## Slide 8: Core Module 6 - Smart Planner & Calendar
*   **AI-Driven Suggestions:** The system suggests what you should study next based on upcoming deadlines.
*   **Calendar View:** A visual schedule of your entire month/week to keep your academic life on track.

---

## Slide 9: Core Module 7 - Reports & Feedback
*   **Easy Submission:** Students can submit their progress reports directly through the platform.
*   **Teacher Interaction:** Teachers can review reports and provide instant feedback to help students improve.

---

## Slide 10: The Technology Stack (Simple Version)
*   **Frontend:** React (for a fast and interactive user interface).
*   **Backend:** FastAPI (to handle data quickly and efficiently).
*   **Database:** MySQL (to safely store all user and course information).
*   **Design:** Custom Vanilla CSS (to create a unique, premium look without generic templates).

---

## Slide 11: Future Enhancements
*   **Mobile App:** Expanding the planner to iOS and Android.
*   **Collaborative Study:** Group study rooms and shared task boards.
*   **AI Tutoring:** Integration of AI to answer subject-specific questions.

---

## Slide 12: Conclusion
*   **Impact:** Simplifies the academic journey.
*   **Efficiency:** Saves time through automation and smart planning.
*   **Scalability:** Built to grow with more courses and more students.
